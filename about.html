ABOUT TEMASWATI

Helping businesses take
their next step.

Practical, accessible support for people building and growing businesses.

Temaswati Business Consultancy is being developed as an online-first business support division of Temaswati Group. Our focus is to help entrepreneurs and small businesses prepare professional documents, organise key business information and navigate selected administrative processes.

We believe that business support should be clear, respectful and practical. We work with clients to understand what they need, agree on the scope of work and explain the next steps before proceeding.
