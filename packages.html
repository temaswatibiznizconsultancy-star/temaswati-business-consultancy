Common Questions

Before You Book.

Are package prices fixed?

The three packages are quotation-based. We confirm the scope, deliverables and final price in writing before work begins.

Are statutory and third-party fees included?

Not automatically. Applicable statutory, government and third-party fees are separate unless your written quotation expressly states otherwise.

Will you guarantee registration or funding?

No. We assist with preparation and administrative steps, but the relevant authority or funder makes the final decision.

When do I pay?

Payment terms will be included in your written quotation and service agreement. Work begins according to the agreed terms.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Tell us what you need, and we will confirm whether it falls within our service scope and provide a written quotation.
